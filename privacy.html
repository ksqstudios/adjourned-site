Adjourned

Privacy

Adjourned is built to know as little as possible. This page describes everything the app touches.

Your calendar

With your permission, Adjourned reads recurring events from your calendar to list your standing meetings. This reading happens entirely on your device. Calendar data is never stored by the app beyond a display-name snapshot, never transmitted to us, and never shared with anyone. Adjourned never writes to your calendar. Calendar access is optional; the app works fully with manually entered meetings.

Your decisions

The reviews and decisions you record are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, synced through your private iCloud database so they follow you across your devices. That data belongs to your Apple ID; we cannot read it.

What we collect

Nothing. Adjourned has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no servers of ours. This website collects nothing either.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Apple. We receive no payment details.
