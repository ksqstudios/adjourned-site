End the meetings you should not be in.

Standing meetings accumulate like subscriptions. Created for a project that shipped or a crisis that passed, recurring forever, because cancelling is awkward and creating was easy. Adjourned counts what they cost, and holds the door while you end the ones that no longer earn their slot.

The honest accounting

01

Read from your calendar

Every recurring series, priced at hours per year and an estimated annual cost. Nothing is written, and nothing leaves your device. No calendar? Enter meetings by hand; the app works either way.

02

The monthly ritual

One card per standing meeting, one question: does this meeting earn its time? Then a decision. Keep, shorten, cut, or defer.

03

Decisions are not actions

A cut meeting still counts until you confirm it actually ended. Adjourned records what you decided, and holds you to it.

04

The record

Every review, every decision, over time. A meeting sliding from Usually to Rarely across three reviews is telling you something before you have admitted it.

The day, burning down or clearing up

The color follows your weekly hours. A lean calendar is clear sky; a meeting-swamped one has gone to sunset.

Side by side

On iPad and on the iPhone Duo's inner display, your stack of standing meetings sits beside the one you are looking at. Ask Siri how many hours your standing meetings take each week, find any meeting in Spotlight, and save the whole record as a spreadsheet.
