Adjourned 1.1

Standing meetings accumulate like subscriptions. Adjourned counts what they cost, and holds the door while you end the ones that no longer earn their slot. Here is what changed in 1.1.

Side by side

On iPad and on the iPhone Duo's inner display, your stack of standing meetings sits beside the one you are looking at. Fold the Duo and whatever was open stays open.

Siri, Shortcuts, and Spotlight

Ask Siri how many hours your standing meetings take each week, start your monthly review, or open any meeting by name. Every meeting is searchable in Spotlight.

A medium widget

Your weekly hours, beside your three heaviest meetings.

Export the record

Every review and decision, saved as a spreadsheet, ready for Numbers or Excel.
